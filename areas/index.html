We come to you

AREAS WE COVER

Free collection and delivery on orders of £40 or more, from Cranleigh down to the coast, between Shoreham and Bognor Regis. Worthing and Shoreham, and inland to Arundel, Steyning and Pulborough.

DO WE COLLECT FROM YOU?

Pop in your postcode and we'll tell you straight away.

HOW COLLECTION WORKS

  • Book online, call or WhatsApp
  • We agree a time to collect from your home or work
  • Your clubs are back within 72 hours for most jobs
  • Free on orders of £40 or more, £5 otherwise
