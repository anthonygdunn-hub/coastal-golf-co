REGRIPS

Worn grips cost you shots before you've even swung. Fresh grips, fitted properly, give you a secure hold with less grip pressure, and that means a freer, more consistent swing.

Buy 6, get the 7th free · back within 72 hours
Book regrips07792 554989

WHAT'S INCLUDED

  • Old grip and tape removed, shaft cleaned back to bare metal or graphite
  • New double-sided grip tape, built up to your chosen size
  • Grip fitted straight and aligned square to the clubface
  • Every club checked and fully set before it comes back to you
  • Honest advice on size and style if you're not sure what suits you

SIGNS IT'S TIME

  • Grips look shiny or feel slippery, especially in the wet
  • Cracks, splits or a worn patch where your thumb sits
  • You find yourself squeezing harder to stop the club turning
  • It's been more than a year, or around 40 rounds, since your last set

CHOOSE YOUR GRIP

RUBBER

Soft, comfortable and tacky. The most popular choice and a great all-rounder.

CORD

Cotton cord woven into the rubber for maximum traction in the wet or on hot days. A firmer feel.

HYBRID

Cord under the top hand, softer rubber under the bottom hand. Traction where you need it, comfort where you want it.

WRAP

A classic wrapped style with a cushioned, traditional feel.

PUTTER

Pistol, paddle and oversize shapes to quieten the hands on the greens.

GETTING THE SIZE RIGHT

Grips come in undersize, standard, midsize and jumbo, and extra layers of tape fine-tune the thickness. Too thin and the hands get busy; too thick and it's hard to release the club. Your glove size is a good starting point and we'll help you with the rest. If you like a chunkier feel, JumboMax and SuperStroke make some of the best oversize grips around. Got a favourite? Just ask.

QUESTIONS

How often should I regrip?

As a rule of thumb, once a year or every 40 rounds, whichever comes first. If you practise a lot or play in all weathers, grips wear faster.

How soon can I play after a regrip?

Straight away. Grips are fitted with a solvent that needs time to set, so they're left to cure before your clubs come back to you, fully set and ready to play.

Can I supply my own grips?

Yes. Fitting only is £4 a club.

How does the 7th free offer work?

Buy six regrips and get the seventh free, on any grips. If you mix grips, the cheapest one is the free one. Regrip fourteen and two are free. The calculator above does the maths for you.

LOFT & LIE

If good swings are finishing offline, or two irons go the same distance, your lofts and lies are the first place to look. Small adjustments, big difference.

Measured before and after
Book loft & lie07792 554989

WHAT'S INCLUDED

  • Every club measured on a loft and lie machine
  • Adjusted to your specification, or to what we recommend
  • Loft gaps checked across the set so every club has its own distance
  • Wedge bounce considered, not just loft
  • Before and after specs written down for your records

SIGNS IT'S NEEDED

  • Straight-looking strikes that keep finishing left or right
  • Two clubs that go the same distance, or a big gap between them
  • A lot of practice off mats, which can knock lofts out over time
  • You've never had them checked

HOW IT WORKS

LIE ANGLE

The angle between the shaft and the ground at address. Too upright and the face points left at impact; too flat and it points right (for a right-handed golfer).

LOFT

Controls height and distance. Consistent gaps mean every club has a job and there are no awkward yardages.

YOUR CLUBS

Forged irons adjust easily. Most cast irons can be adjusted within a smaller range. Many hybrids and woods can't be bent at all. We always check first.

DONE SAFELY

Every head is checked for material and construction before it goes near the machine, and no club is ever pushed beyond what it will safely take.

QUESTIONS

How often should lofts and lies be checked?

Once a year is sensible for regular golfers, more often if you practise a lot off mats.

Will adjusting damage my clubs?

Done properly and within the club's limits, no. We check the head first and never force a club beyond what it will safely take.

Can you adjust my putter?

Yes, in most cases. Putter loft and lie make a real difference to how the ball starts and rolls.

The 8th, a par 3 that's tough in any conditions. Get on the wrong side of the green and three putts or more are guaranteed. Also the par 4 1st, one of the toughest opening holes in the South East.
Best memory
Winning the 36-hole County Club Cup in 1976, my first competition win, at 15 years old. Then a hole in one on the 16th in September 1984.
Top tip
Beware of the rife meandering through the course: it comes into play on six of the holes. There are some nasty little bunkers too.

ROYAL BIRKDALE

Fairways framed by giant dunes

In 1998 I was working for TaylorMade at the Open when I first met my now amazing wife of 25 years, and plucked up the courage to get a friend to get her number. She worked for Asprey & Garrard, the Crown Jewellers, who were exhibiting opposite. Her colleagues got a lot of discounts that week! I also met a lifelong friend that week, who has been amazing throughout this time.

Favourite hole
The 18th. What a great finishing hole, with wonderful drama over the years: Seve's genius chip in 1976, Watson's second shot in 1983 to secure the title, then Justin Rose's chip-in to finish fourth and win the silver medal for the best amateur.
Best memory
Watching Craig Stadler hit his second shot on the 9th, slam his club into his bag and say some naughty words, then walk onto the green, hole his 10-foot birdie putt as calm as anything and stroll to the next tee. Easy game!
Top tip
Probably the toughest Open venue. Give it lots of respect: any score close to your handicap is very respectable.

HAYLING

Sea air and shifting winds

I had the privilege of working in the office for nine months in 2023, between general managers. I had played once before, in the '80s, and couldn't remember just how good Hayling is. More than that, I met so many lovely people who are great friends now.

Favourite hole
There are so many at Hayling, but the 10th through to the 13th has everything for me in a golf course: the risk-and-reward 10th; the superb par 3 11th, a card wrecker if ever there was one; the magnificent par 4 12th, the toughest hole on the course; then the 13th, the signature hole. But the rest are superb too.
Best memory
The friendliness of the members, when I worked there and ever since. Also winning a nine-hole match 3 & 1.
Top tip
A must-play if you're in the area, any time of year. It has to be the best-draining course in the UK. You're guaranteed a warm welcome from everyone, great food and a superb test of golf. But be patient: it's a links course, remember.

ABERDOVEY

Links on the Welsh coast

I first played Aberdovey during the hot summer of 1976, on a caravan holiday with Mum and Dad. The next time was 1998, when I was out on the road with TaylorMade: the pro there was my last call on a Friday morning every two months, and I fell in love with the course and the town. I set up a golf tour, the Horny Aber DelBoy Challenge, and have been back some 20-plus times since with good friends and customers. Wonderful memories.

Favourite hole
The signature 12th, a par 3. A third of the green was wiped out a few years ago and had to be rebuilt, but it's been beautifully done. It's a great test of nerve.
Best memory
As well as every HADC trip, my second hole in one, on the par 3 5th in 2023. It was actually a great shot, and I managed to win that hole!
Top tip
It's a long way from anywhere, but once you get there you'll be totally chilled. Whatever the weather forecast says, Aberdovey is protected by the hills around it, so the course is playable all year round. Just don't get complacent: yes, it's flat, and to some it may look uninteresting, but it can eat you up very easily.

The Old Course, St Andrews, Fife

ST ANDREWS

The Home of Golf

The Old Course is the one they all talk about, but in my opinion it's not the best test. The New and the Jubilee are superb, and the Castle is great but a bit quirky. However, the Old starts and finishes in the town, which oozes golf: an incredible atmosphere all year round, with so many nationalities, whether visitors or the university.

Favourite hole
The Old is all about the 1st, 17th and 18th. When you stand on that first tee, you're not human if you don't get goosebumps, and the same when you finish on the last. And the 17th, what a hole: over the hotel to a long but narrowest of greens.
Best memory
When I was at Callaway Golf, I set up a global licence agreement, and over those years I had the privilege of going to St Andrews at least three times a year. Because of that, I was invited to play on the Monday after Tiger won in 2005. It took six hours to play, but driving the 18th, ending up in the Valley of Sin and two-putting for a birdie was the cherry on top of a most memorable round.
Top tip
If you're a serious golfer and have never played golf in Scotland, then shame on you, even more so if you haven't been to St Andrews.

COLLECTION & TURNAROUND

01How long will I be without my clubs?

Most jobs are back with you within 72 hours of collection. If a part needs ordering, such as a particular shaft, we'll tell you how long before we start.

02Do you really come and collect?

Yes. We collect from your home or your golf club and bring your clubs back when they're done. It's free on orders of £40 or more, and £5 otherwise.

03Which areas do you cover?

From Cranleigh down to the coast: north to Cranleigh, east to Shoreham and west to Bognor Regis, taking in Horsham, Billingshurst, Pulborough, Steyning, Worthing, Littlehampton and Arundel. Use the postcode checker on our Areas page to check yours in seconds.

05I've got a round this weekend. Can you help?

Yes. Our express service can often turn clubs round the same day. Call us on 07792 554989 for details.

GRIPS

06Can you fit grips I've already bought?

Yes. Fitting only is £4 a club.

07How often should I regrip?

Once a year or every 40 rounds, whichever comes first. If grips look shiny, feel slippery or you find yourself squeezing harder, it's time.

08What grip size do I need?

Your glove size is the best starting point, and our grip size guide on the Regrips page will suggest one. Feel matters more than any chart, so we're happy to talk it through when we collect.

09How soon can I play after a regrip?

Straight away. Grips are left to set properly before they come back to you, so they're ready for your next round.

10How does the 7th regrip free work?

Buy six regrips and get the seventh free, on any grips. If you mix grips, the cheapest one is the free one. The calculator on our Services page does the maths for you.

REPAIRS & ADJUSTMENTS

11How much can loft and lie be changed?

It depends on the club. Forged irons can usually be bent two or three degrees safely. Cast irons are more limited, often around one degree. Many hybrids and woods can't be bent at all. We check every head before it goes near the machine and never push a club beyond what it will safely take.

12Can you shorten or lengthen my clubs?

Yes, for £8 a club. We check the swingweight afterwards, because changing the length changes how the head feels, and can add weight to bring the feel back. We also cut down and extend junior clubs as they grow.

14Is it worth repairing an older club?

Often, yes, especially a club you trust. If a repair isn't worth doing, we'll tell you honestly before you spend anything.

15What's in a Club MOT?

Every club checked: grips, loft and lie, shafts, heads, ferrules, lengths and grooves. You get a written report in plain English and a quote for anything we recommend. The £30 fee is refunded in full if you go ahead with work over £100.

PRICES, PAYMENT & DISCOUNTS

16Will I know the price before you start?

Always. Every job is priced before any work begins, and nothing is done without your OK.

18Who gets a discount?

All juniors under 17 get 20% off every service except custom logo designs.

19Do you work with clubs and societies?

Yes, with group orders: regrips for a whole team or society, or logo balls for a society day. Juniors get 20% off. See our Clubs & societies page.

PRIVACY

How we collect, use and look after your information. Last updated October 2026.

WHO WE ARE

Coastal Golf Co is a golf club repair and care service based in Cranleigh, covering Cranleigh down to the West Sussex coast. If you have any questions about your information, email hello@coastalgolfco.co.uk or call 07792 554989.

WHAT WE COLLECT

When you book or get in touch, we ask for your name, phone number, email address, collection postcode and address, and details about your clubs and what they need. We only collect what we need to look after your clubs.

WHY WE USE IT

To arrange collection and delivery, do the work you've asked for, keep you updated, and invoice you. We rely on this being necessary to provide the service you've requested. We will never sell your details, and we won't send you marketing unless you've asked us to.

HOW IT REACHES US

The booking form opens an email or WhatsApp message on your own device, which you then send to us. Your details go straight to our inbox or phone; nothing is stored by this website. Email and WhatsApp messages are handled by those providers under their own privacy policies.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep booking details while we're working on your clubs and for as long as we need them for our records. Invoices and payment records are kept for six years, as HMRC requires. After that they're deleted.

COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking, and doesn't collect any information about you unless you choose to contact us. The site is hosted by GitHub, which keeps basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service running securely.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Just email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
